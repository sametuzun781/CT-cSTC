ICORE augmented recovery adaptation

Source provenance: QOCO commit 598de4e1aeaa9c522b3bc5bb1a56f3d7287dc14f,
including its vendored AMD/SuiteSparse_config and QDLDL. Original copyright,
SPDX, license texts and historical modification notices are retained.

AMD/SuiteSparse_config: symbols receive a private icore_chain_ prefix; NTIMER
is defined; amd_order.c has five explicit allocation casts for C++17.
The ordering algorithm is unchanged.

QDLDL: exported symbols receive a private icore_chain_ prefix. The two dynamic
pivot replacement blocks added by the QOCO adaptation are removed. Thus this
copy performs no dynamic diagonal perturbation, pivot clipping or tolerance
replacement. Its historical source comment "DYNAMIC REGULARIZATION ADDED"
describes the upstream QOCO modification, not the behavior of this copy.
ICORE rejects nonfinite, zero or incorrectly signed pivots/inverse pivots and
retains its original QR recovery if the exact operator cannot be represented.
